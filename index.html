Where the time usually goes

  • Requests arrive missing a detail — the certificate holder’s full name and address, the additional insured wording, or the dates. Someone has to go back to the requester or the insured before anything can be issued.
  • The certificate goes out with the wrong limits, entity name or address, and the whole thing is reissued. That is the round trip that never shows up on a timesheet.
  • Chasing the answer — following up with the insured, the lender or the certificate holder, often more than once, before the request can be closed.
